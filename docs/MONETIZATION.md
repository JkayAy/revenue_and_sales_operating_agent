# Monetization Plan

**Product:** PipelinePilot  
**Model:** B2B SaaS + usage (portfolio + optional live Stripe stub)

---

## 1. Pricing tiers

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Starter** | $49/user/mo | 1 HubSpot portal, 500 agent runs/mo, approval queue |
| **Team** | $99/user/mo | Manager metrics, playbook editor, 2,000 runs |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | SSO, Salesforce connector, SLA, VPC |

**Overage:** $0.05 per agent run (qualify + enrich + research + draft).

---

## 2. What counts as a run

One **lead_run** from ingest through draft (retries on same idempotency key do not double-bill).

---

## 3. ROI framing (interviews)

- 200 inbound leads/month × 15 min saved × $45/hr ≈ **$2,250/mo** labor  
- Software at 10 seats Team ≈ **$990/mo**  
- Net illustrative savings ≈ **$1,260/mo** before faster close rates  

---

## 4. Marketplace (future)

Vertical **playbook packs** (fintech ICP, healthcare compliance tone) — 70% author / 30% platform.
